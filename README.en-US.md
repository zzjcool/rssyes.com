# RSSYES

> 🎧 Turn YouTube into Your Podcast Radio | Professional RSS Subscription Generation Service

## ✨ Introduction

RSSYES is an RSS subscription service focused on YouTube content aggregation. It converts YouTube video channels into standard podcast (Podcast) RSS feeds, allowing you to listen to high-quality content during your commute or work breaks through universal podcast clients (such as Xiaomi Podcast, Apple Podcasts) in the background.

🌐 **Website:** [https://rssyes.com](https://rssyes.com)

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

### Q: What should I do if the channel I want to listen to is not in the homepage list?
**A:** You can convert it yourself now!
Just visit [YouTube to Podcast Tool](https://rssyes.com/youtube-to-podcast), enter the channel link to generate.

### Q: What is the difference between the homepage's "Curated Channels" and "Self-Service Conversion"?
- **Curated Channels**: High-quality content manually selected and collected by us, publicly displayed on the homepage, suitable for exploring new content.
- **Self-Service Conversion**: Suitable for when you have a specific target channel you want to listen to, or the content is niche/private and you don't want it publicly displayed.
